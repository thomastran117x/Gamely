# Contributor Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct is based on Contributor Covenant 3.0 and applies to all Games project spaces.

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation welcoming, safe, and equitable. Everyone who participates in good
faith and follows this Code of Conduct receives the same opportunity to contribute, regardless of
identity, background, ability, experience, appearance, beliefs, or personal circumstances.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- engage honestly, kindly, and with respect for the project's purpose;
- consider how words and actions may be received across cultures and experiences;
- welcome constructive disagreement and feedback;
- take responsibility for mistakes and work to repair harm; and
- protect other people's privacy and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not permitted:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or deliberately violating a stated boundary;
- insults, demeaning comments, character attacks, or sustained disruption;
- sexualized conduct or unwelcome sexual attention;
- disclosing or threatening to disclose another person's private information;
- encouraging others to violate this Code of Conduct; or
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in its review.

Good-faith criticism of a contribution is not a personal attack. Enforcement decisions should
consider context, impact, patterns of behavior, and opportunities to repair harm.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, commits, and other project-managed
spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents Games in public or private spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/thomastran117). Do not publish sensitive reports in
an issue or pull request.

Include the relevant links, dates, context, and any immediate safety concern. Reports will be
handled as privately as reasonably possible. Information may be shared only with people needed to
review the report, protect participants, or meet legal obligations. Knowingly false or retaliatory
reports are themselves violations; an unsubstantiated good-faith report is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, issue warnings, or
temporarily or permanently ban a participant. The response will be proportionate to the severity,
impact, recurrence, and willingness to repair harm. When safe and appropriate, maintainers will
explain the violated standard and any conditions for restored participation.

Anyone responsible for handling a report must disclose conflicts of interest and step away when an
impartial review is not possible.
